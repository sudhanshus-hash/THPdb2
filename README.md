# THPdb2: Compilation of FDA Approved Therapeutic Peptides and Proteins

Welcome to the official repository and documentation overview for **THPdb2**, an updated and significantly expanded version of the original THPdb. This manually curated database serves as a comprehensive knowledge base for all types of US-FDA approved protein-based drugs, including peptides, polypeptides, monoclonal antibodies, and biosimilar proteins.

**Web Server:** [https://webs.iiitd.edu.in/raghava/thpdb2/](https://webs.iiitd.edu.in/raghava/thpdb2/)

## About the Database

THPdb2 was developed to provide an integrated resource for the rapidly growing field of protein therapeutics. In the seven years since the inception of the original THPdb, the number of FDA-approved protein drugs has increased significantly. THPdb2 contains three times more information than its predecessor, helping researchers annotate and understand the therapeutic potential of proteins.

The database integrates data from:
* **Primary Literature:** Meticulously collected and curated from research publications.
* **External Repositories:** Data sourced from **DrugBank**, **PubChem**, and **UniProt**.

## Key Features

### Comprehensive Dataset
* **894 unique therapeutic molecules** approved by the FDA.
* **6,385 total entries** providing a massive expansion over the original version.
* **Specific Categories:** Includes 354 monoclonal antibodies and 85 peptides or polypeptides.
* **Broad Therapeutic Range:** Covers treatments for cancers, infectious diseases, immunological disorders, and metabolic disorders.

### Rich Annotations
Each record includes:
* **Sequence Information:** Full amino acid sequences for each therapeutic protein.
* **Molecular Properties:** Detailed physical and chemical properties of the molecules.
* **Clinical Data:** Specific information on the target biological molecules (receptors, factors) and the route of drug administration.

### Built-in Tools
* **Search Modules:** User-friendly basic and advanced search modules based on parameters like molecular weight, category, drug target, and brand name.
* **Similarity Search:** Integrated tools to allow users to explore datasets based on sequence similarity.
* **Responsive Interface:** Developed using HTML5 and PHP5 for compatibility across mobile phones, tablets, and computers.


## Overview

THPdb2 is built on a robust architecture to manage extensive pharmacological data:
1.  **Search Module:** Supports Boolean expressions (AND, OR, NOT) for complex data mining.
2.  **Administration Routes:** Detailed documentation of the various ways these therapeutic proteins are delivered to patients.
3.  **Target Activity:** Mapping of drugs to their specific biological targets and mechanisms.



## Applications

* **Therapeutic Discovery:** Screening novel proteins for therapeutic potential by comparing them to successfully approved drugs.
* **Structural Bioinformatics:** Utilizing sequence and property data to understand protein-drug interactions.
* **Market Analysis:** Tracking the evolution of the protein/peptide therapeutic market, which is projected to reach billions in global revenue by 2030.
